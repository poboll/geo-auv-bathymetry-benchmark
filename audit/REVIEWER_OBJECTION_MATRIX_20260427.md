## Status Answer

The experiment has succeeded as a numerical benchmark. The evidence chain is:

- two real public GEBCO gridded bathymetry subsets,
- three synthetic mechanism/stress-test scenes,
- 20-seed GA runs for stochastic methods,
- target-overlap and grid-resolution sensitivity diagnostics,
- a clean LaTeX before build archived as `latex/compile_before_reviewer_repair_20260426.log`.

The experiment has not produced AUV sea-trial logs, raw MBES returns, or field-executed tracks. The manuscript must therefore keep using the wording "public-grid numerical benchmark" rather than "field validation".
